Maps of Irish administrative geography and history ¦ Léarscáileanna de thíreolaíocht riaracháin agus de stair na hÉireann ¦
Civgraph brings together the civic record of Ireland, tied to people, places and events. ¦ Tugann Civgraph taifead cathartha na hÉireann le chéile, ceangailte le daoine, le háiteanna agus le himeachtaí. ¦
Boundaries, elections, people and documents, from the 1830s to the present, drawn from public records, archives and original digitisation, and free to use with attribution under the licences each source sets. ¦ Teorainneacha, toghcháin, daoine agus doiciméid, ó na 1830idí go dtí an lá atá inniu ann, bunaithe ar thaifid phoiblí, ar chartlanna agus ar bhundigitiú, agus saor le húsáid le sannadh faoi na ceadúnais a leagann gach foinse síos. ¦
Every boundary layer on one interactive map: wards, districts, constituencies, townlands, baronies, civil parishes - with election results and census data painted onto the boundaries that actually existed at the time. ¦ Gach sraith teorann ar aon léarscáil idirghníomhach amháin: bardaí, ceantair, toghcheantair, bailte fearainn, barúntachtaí, paróistí sibhialta - le torthaí toghchán agus sonraí daonáirimh curtha ar na teorainneacha a bhí ann ag an am. ¦
