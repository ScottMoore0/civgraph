The Belfast Gazette

The Gazette (The Stationery Office) · Crown copyright, Open Government Licence · source

About 9,400 issues known. The Gazette numbers its Belfast issues consecutively from the first, in 1921; about 9,400 have been published. The figure is the issue range Civgraph's crawler is set to cover, kept by hand.

  • Downloaded: 5,731 (about 61%)
  • Read for election and boundary notices: 4,757 (about 51%)
  • Cited on the site: 221 (about 2.4%)
  • Downloaded so far: issues 1 to 6,310; 579 of them have no copy online.
  • Notices found so far: 787 boundary notices and 185 election notices, in issues 1 to 5335.
  • Cited on the site counts the different issues that map and election records cite.

Issues are downloaded oldest first, so the early decades are done first.
