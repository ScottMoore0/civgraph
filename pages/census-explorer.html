Census Explorer

An interactive view over the cleaned Northern Ireland census query model: which concepts are available across the 2001, 2011 and 2021 digital censuses and in the historical reports (1841–1991), and a finder over the logical NISRA source tables. Only concepts and tables that resolve to a real public source are shown; gaps are marked unavailable.
