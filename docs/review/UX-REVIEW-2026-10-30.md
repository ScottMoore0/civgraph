# Site-wide UI/UX review — 2026-10-30

This review covers every publicly reachable page. It was run against the live site with
Playwright in Chromium, at desktop 1440, tablet 820 and phone 390 widths (plus a 320 spot
check), in light and dark modes. It also used axe-core, throttled and offline runs,
keyboard-only passes, bad URLs and timings.

Areas that had gone longest without review were weighted first. Those came from git
history and from `docs/review/UX-TRIAGE.md` (last triaged 2026-08-23).

There are 73 findings in all:
- 14 high
- 35 medium
- 24 low

## Map app (/maps/): other findings

### Medium
- **No legend** for election fills, seat dots or boundary layers. FF and SF are both green.
- **Timeline ticks sit at the wrong dates.** "1950" marks the 1955 edition and "1970" marks 1977, because the slider is index-based. The Westminster axis is crowded at the right end.
- **The timeline drops the constituency.** Prev and Next lose the selected constituency.
- **Views don't fit the viewer's screen.** Shared links carry the sender's zoom, so phone recipients see only part of the layer. Opening Dáil 2020 leaves Great Britain filling half the map.
- **Clicked boundary features aren't in the URL.** `selected` exists but is unused.
- **STV chart:**
  - 28px stage numbers with a 3px black outline;
  - "Quota" overlaps the stage numbers on phones;
  - 7 stage buttons against 6 stages.
- **Election pane labels:**
  - only abbreviations even at 1440px;
  - "East Lo…" truncation on phones;
  - NI local is titled "Local Government Districts";
  - Assembly and local polls are labelled "General election".
- **Map Settings shows developer controls:**
  - a "Performance" debug panel;
  - "Fill Transparency 100%", which means invisible;
  - a USGS basemap that is blank for Ireland;
  - an emoji.
- **Map controls are unclear.** The Active Layers toggle is a bare caret; settings is a gear with "Aa"; phones have two catalogue toggles.
- **Map labels:**
  - Republic baronies in upper case, NI in title case;
  - collisions;
  - basemap labels duplicate layer labels;
  - low-contrast cyan.

  File: `render/src/labels.js`.
- **Dark mode keeps a bright-white basemap.** Dimming it with raster brightness and saturation is cheap.
- **No constituency picker in elections.** The only way in is the map or a candidate row.
- **Accessibility markup:**
  - `#splitDrag` isn't focusable;
  - the pane resizer lacks `aria-valuenow`;
  - the seat overlay has no role;
  - the live announcer never says "loaded".

### Low
- **Catalogue lists:**
  - the "72 series, 287 maps." line is clipped;
  - "0 dated maps" appears beside results;
  - Places results show blank thumbnails.
- **Zoom controls:** the zoom-out button uses a hyphen, and the controls are 30px on phones.
- **Change values:**
  - constituency % changes lack a "%";
  - Detailed View shows −100.00% for a surplus.
- **Feedback:** Copy link gives no confirmation, and a slow load shows a blank map with no spinner.
- **Errors on every load:**
  - `/_api/contributions/whoami` errors on every load (it redirects to Access);
  - NI local 2023, 2019 and 2014 log provenance 404s.
- **Missing features:**
  - no print stylesheet;
  - `?q=` is ignored (only `#q=` works).
- **Tablet portrait** keeps the split, leaving the map about 415px wide.
- **Feature header** reads "Counties of Ireland 1927 (all-island, Donegal)" over Roscommon; "Part area" is jargon.
- **Data:** Dáil 2020 shows no previous result for S-PBP (it stood in 2016 as AAA-PBP).

### Works well
- skip links and visible focus rings;
- Escape returns focus;
- deep links restore elections;
- no horizontal overflow on phones;
- fast loads: catalogue interactive in 1.0–1.6s, first layer in 0.6–1.2s (Townlands 2.5s).

## Rest of site: other findings

The page inventory has 29 public page types or routes, 15 of them Browse hash-route types:
- **Main pages:** /, /browse/, /records/, /catalogue/, /apps/.
- **PRONI:** /proni and /proni/&lt;ref&gt;.
- **Pages:** /pages/about.html and /pages/census-explorer.html.
- **Special pages:** 404 and offline.
- **Shared chrome:** header, mobile menu, footer and support modal.
- **Technical:** /data/elections-source/demo.html, /render/, robots.txt, llms.txt and /agent/*.md.
- **Missing:** sitemap.xml.

### Medium
- **About 70 Browse portal sub-links are fake filters.** Townlands, Referendums and Seats won all go to the unfiltered list.
- **Browse search does nothing on the landing pages** ("Choose a section to search"), and the header has no search. Relabel (S); a cross-type search is M.
- **Detail pages download 5.7–7.9MB of indexes.** On throttled 4G a person takes 8s to show content and an election 10.5s. Per-entity shards or an API would fix it (L).
- **Homepage map:** 19 focusable city points inside an image, with no focus ring.
- **PRONI app:**
  - no dark mode and no site nav, and a different typeface;
  - the Apps card stays light in dark mode;
  - the query isn't kept in the URL;
  - date fields are clipped at 390px.
- **Counts disagree.** Browse says "Elections 12,527" while About and the homepage say 1,135 elections and 10,257 contests. Lists say "1 constituencies".
- **stormont-1929 feature group:**
  - its spatial index is a 404, so the list is missing and the page never finishes;
  - it reports "27,464 features" for 52 seats;
  - developer copy ("spatial-index sidecars");
  - pc-1948 shows names in ALL CAPS with raw coordinates.
- **Sources list** opens on ": Public survey…" and three duplicate "Make Inroads" records.
- **Persons list:** 200 identical "Open" buttons that go to the map, tall cards, no facets.
- **Section naming:** "Browse the catalogue" goes to a page headed "data directory"; /records/ and /catalogue/ are in neither nav; there are two PRONI interfaces.
- **Person facts:** Gerry Adams's elected bodies omit the Dáil, and his 1992 loss shows a blank status.
- **Person → constituency path leaves Browse.** Election names link to /maps/ and constituencies aren't linked.

### Low
- **About page:** no meta description, a skipped heading level, and 9.5k px with no table of contents.
- **PRONI record page:**
  - empty rows shown;
  - a cryptic pager "[2275 – 9404]";
  - footer links that just say "here";
  - two clear buttons;
  - a footer layout shift of 0.11.
- **Offline page** ignores the saved theme.
- **`demo.html`** is public, unbranded and unlabelled, with no noindex.
- **The Contributor box and Log in button** come before the H1 for anonymous visitors.
- **Footer is thin:** no Apps, PRONI, Records, licence or privacy links, and no year after ©.
- **Homepage counters** start at 0, so a slow or no-JS view says "0 public maps".
- **`/404` returns 200.**
- **Records and Catalogue intro links** aren't underlined (2.04:1 against the text).
- **Mobile menu:** the theme toggle sits on its own row.

### Works well
- the support modal: labelled, focus trapped, Escape returns focus;
- the theme persists across pages;
- no horizontal overflow at 390px;
- PRONI search is fast (0.45s on throttled 4G), with good empty and error states.

## Covered thinly

- **Map app:** Trends and By Local Party, referendum, presidential and European elections, bye-elections, Conditional Styling and overlays, raster and point-cloud layers, the Statistics and Places tabs, touch gestures.
- **Not tested at all:** Safari and Firefox, and a real screen reader.
- **Rest of site:** register-interest filters (the list is broken), PRONI advanced fields and CSV export, the deeper #/proni tree, the service-worker offline fallback, print, /render/, and the contributor login and submission flows.
