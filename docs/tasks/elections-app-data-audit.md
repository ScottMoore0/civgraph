## Totals

- 2,368 elections (898 by-elections, 50 referendums or recall petitions) across 98 bodies; 2,130 name a previous election (`previous_key`).
- Voting systems: fptp 2,080, stv-hare 180, stv-gregory 57, ordinal 50, party-list-dhondt 1.
- 21,010 contests; 100% have candidate rows, 45% have votes, 16% record the count at which candidates were elected or excluded, and 11% have stage-by-stage totals with transfers (a count padded to several stages with unchanged totals, as some Irish scraper sources are, counts as one stage).
- Electorate on 40%, turnout on 21%, quota on 20%, valid poll on 46%, spoiled ballots on 22%, seats on 100% of contests.
- 11,434 contests are unopposed returns (no votes; an "Elected unopposed" candidacy, or an uncontested election).
- 76,994 candidacies: party label on 84% (the rest "No party recorded" or blank); 100% of labels fold to a party record through party_label_map; person link on 95%; gender on 2%.
- Provenance per contest: verified 85%, cited 12%, recorded 3%, none 0%.

Column key: **Cands** contests with candidate rows; **Votes** contests with any vote figure; **Count order** contests that record the count at which candidates were elected or excluded; **Stage totals** contests with real stage-by-stage totals (transfers recorded or totals changing between stages); **Electorate**, **Turnout** contests with that figure; **Unopposed** number of unopposed contests; **Party label** candidacies with a label other than "No party recorded"; **Label→party** labelled candidacies whose label folds to a party record; **Person** candidacies linked to a person record; **Gender** candidacies with M/F recorded; the last column is the share of contests whose provenance status is verified / cited / recorded.

## What each view can show (decisions, 11 Nov 2026)

These follow from the figures above. Each view tests what a contest or election actually holds
and degrades rather than inventing figures.

### Election overview (`/elections/<election-slug>/`)

- **Seats and votes by party** come from the election detail's `partySummary`
  (`/_api/elections?view=detail`): seats, candidates stood, votes, share. Shown for every
  election with candidate rows (100%).
- **Votes and share are hidden, not zeroed,** when no contest of the election has votes (45% of
  contests have votes; most pre-1950 NI council and pre-1830 Westminster contests are unopposed
  returns). An election that is partly unopposed shows "votes in N of M contests" beside the
  vote totals, and the share is labelled as the share of votes cast in contested seats.
- **Change versus previous** uses `previous_key` (2,130 of 2,368 elections). Seat change is shown
  whenever both elections have seats; vote-share change only when both have votes.
- **Seat chart:** a parliament arc for elections of 30 or more seats, a seat bar otherwise. Party
  colours from the records (`colour`), with the same figures as a table beside the chart.
- **Turnout** is shown only where contests carry turnout. It is present on 21% of contests
  overall: 98–100% for NI local government (1973–), the Assembly and the Dáil since 2000, rarely
  before. Elsewhere the overview says "not recorded".
- **Leaders:** the records have no party-leader table, so the overview shows none (a later data
  task).
- **Unopposed returns** are listed as "Returned unopposed" with the seat counted and no vote
  figures (never "0" or "0.00%", the QA defect H4 of 11 Nov 2026). The app reads them as
  `packages/core/src/unopposed.mjs` does (an unopposed status, or 0 votes on such a row; the
  Dáil's auto-returned Ceann Comhairle is "Returned automatically"). The election summaries
  write 0, not null, for a contest without a poll, so 0 valid votes means "no poll".

### Contest page (`/elections/<election-slug>/<contest-slug>/`)

- **Full count sheet** where the contest has stage totals (11% of contests: NI local government
  1973– at 98%, the Assembly at 97%, the Dáil 1920s–1960s and 2000s–2020s at 84–96%, the
  European Parliament (NI) at 100%). One column per count, transfers signed, elected and excluded
  marked.
- **Count order only** (the count at which candidates were elected or excluded, but no stage
  totals; about 5% of contests, mainly Irish local elections and the European Parliament
  (Ireland), whose sources pad the count with unchanged totals): first preferences plus "elected
  at count N / excluded at count N"; no fabricated totals, no transfer diagram.
- **Transfer flow diagram:** only with stage totals. The records hold each candidate's net
  transfer at each stage, not candidate-to-candidate flows, so the diagram draws each stage from
  the candidate(s) distributed (negative transfer) to the candidates who gained and to
  non-transferable. It never infers more than that.
- **Quota progress** bars where a quota is recorded (20% of contests; STV only).
- **First past the post:** votes, share and majority; turnout and electorate where held.
- **Unopposed contests:** the returned members, marked "Returned unopposed"; no table of zeros.
- **Sources per figure:** the contest's citations (`view=citations` of `/_api/elections`, added
  on main for QA H5 and extended here with each citation's scope, match and `checkNote`, from
  the `citations` table: title, publisher, link, archive link, kind, basis, whether checked) and
  its provenance status (verified 85%, cited 12%, recorded 3%). Each source
  shows its `check` text, which says which figures it covers ("every figure read from ...",
  "names the candidates, vote figures not found"), so a reader can see which source stands
  behind the votes, the electorate and the count.

### Pivot tables (`/elections/pivot/`)

- One row per candidacy (all candidacies of elections; referendums excluded): dimensions
  election, body, kind, voting system, year, decade, area, candidate, person id, party (ballot
  label, with the folded party slug), gender, incumbency, outcome, unopposed; measures votes,
  share, final votes, transfers received, seats, stages, electorate, turnout, quota, valid poll.
- **Gender** is recorded on 2% of candidacies (the Dáil since 2016 and a few others): offered as
  a dimension with "unknown" shown, never imputed.
- **Incumbency** is derived (there is no source field): the person sat for the same body before
  the election (elected at its previous general election or at a later by-election). Labelled
  "incumbent (derived)". Person links are at 95% or better for every body, so the undercount is
  small.
- **Party** is the ballot label as recorded; 16% of candidacies have "No party recorded".

### Maps and comparisons (`/elections/map/`, `/elections/compare/`, `/elections/swing/`)

- Choropleths only for elections whose contests match map features (`contest_features.matched`
  and the election's `layerId`). Winner and turnout per area; share for a chosen party where
  votes exist; swing between two elections only on the same layer (different boundaries make
  per-area swing meaningless; the notional view handles those).
- The swingometer applies uniform swing to vote shares (first past the post: winner by
  plurality; STV: a quota-based seat estimate, labelled as an estimate).

### Notional results (`/elections/notional/`)

- Areal interpolation where both boundary layers are available; population weighting needs
