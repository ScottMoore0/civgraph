Independent QUB Catalogue

Search the catalogue of Queen’s University Belfast Library for the civic history of Ireland: law, parliaments, elections, local government, places and maps, population, political history and reference works.

Local preview, not published. Every record links back to its record in QUB Library’s catalogue.

    Catalogue records from Queen’s University Belfast Library, reused under QUB’s metadata reuse policy: reuse for not-for-profit purposes, with a link back to each record in QUB’s catalogue. Some records derive from shared cataloguing sources such as OCLC WorldCat. Civgraph is independent of Queen’s University Belfast; this app is not a QUB service. “Within Civgraph scope” is Civgraph’s own label, made by rules and review.
