Census Explorer

Find census tables for Northern Ireland (1926 to 2021) and the Republic of Ireland (1841 to 2022), see the figures here, download them, follow them across censuses, and open the boundaries they were counted on in the map.

    Topics across censuses (Northern Ireland)

    Fifteen census topics, the table that carries each one in 2001, 2011 and 2021, and the earlier censuses that asked about it. Select a table code to see its figures; open a topic for its sources, the earlier reports, and the figures for Northern Ireland as a whole over time.

    Northern Ireland census tables

    Every table in NISRA's 2001 and 2011 bulk releases and the 2021 ready-made tables, with the titles NISRA gave them. Tables marked Figures here can be read on this page from Civgraph's copy.

    Census 2021 flexible tables (Northern Ireland)

    NISRA's flexible table builder releases a table for each combination of up to five questions that passes its disclosure rules: 147,492 tables, all held by Civgraph. Choose the people or household questions, the geography and a question; then narrow the list and open a table.

    Census of Population, Republic of Ireland (CSO)

    The CSO publishes the census as statistical tables ("cubes") on PxStat. These are the census cubes only: each one is listed under the census years it holds, and search reads the questions each cube is broken down by, so religion finds every cube with a religion breakdown.

    Compare over time

    Boundaries move between censuses, so a figure for "the same place" in two years often needs re-basing. Northern Ireland as a whole needs none. The council and small-area comparisons below say which values are as published and which are estimated onto a fixed set of areas, and how.
