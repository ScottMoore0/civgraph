Accessibility statement

How accessible Civgraph is, what we know is not yet right, and how to tell us about a problem.

Using Civgraph

This statement applies to civgraph.net: the maps, Browse and Records, the Catalogue, the Independent PRONI Search, the Census Explorer, Words in parliament and the other apps on the site.

Civgraph is run by a small group of volunteers, and we want as many people as possible to be able to use it. For example, you should be able to:

  • change colours, contrast levels and fonts using your browser or device settings;
  • zoom in up to 400% without the text spilling off the screen (the interactive map itself excepted);
  • get from the top of any page to its main content with a "Skip to main content" link;
  • navigate most of the website using just a keyboard or speech recognition software;
  • listen to most of the website using a screen reader;
  • use the site in a light or dark theme: it follows your device's setting, and the moon and sun button switches it;
  • turn off animation: we follow your device's "reduce motion" setting.

We have also tried to make the text as simple as we can, given that much of the site is a record of historical documents. AbilityNet has advice on making your device easier to use if you have a disability.

What is not yet accessible

We know some parts of this website are not fully accessible:

  • The interactive map is a picture drawn by your browser. You can move it with the arrow keys and zoom with + and −, but a screen reader cannot read the shapes on it. The same information is available as text through the map's search box, a map's own page in Browse (which lists every feature in a table) and the election pages (which give every result as a table).
  • Many sources are scans of old documents and maps, from PRONI, the Internet Archive, newspapers and printed books. We transcribe the figures we use into tables, but the scans themselves are images.
  • Some small controls on the map, such as the close button on the feature panel and the timeline controls, are smaller than recommended for touch and pointer use.
  • On the map pages, the two "Skip to" links are hidden behind the page header when you tab to them, although they still work.
  • In the map catalogue, some buttons can scroll under a section heading as you tab through them, and the feature panel can cover the "Active layers" button.
  • In the PRONI search, opening a record from the results does not move keyboard focus to the record, the quick-view box does not keep keyboard focus inside it, and some filter buttons do not say which one is selected.
  • In parts of Browse and Records, links inside sentences are shown by colour only and are not underlined.
  • One selected-layer button in the map catalogue has white text on orange with too little contrast.

Feedback and contact information

If you need information on this website in a different format, tell us. For example, you can ask for a table as a CSV file or as plain text, a description of what a map shows, or a transcription of a scanned source we hold.

Email Scott@civgraph.net. We will consider your request and aim to reply within one month.

Reporting accessibility problems

We are always looking to improve the accessibility of this website. If you find a problem not listed on this page, or think we are not meeting accessibility requirements, email Scott@civgraph.net. Please tell us the page address, what you were trying to do, and the browser, device and any assistive technology you use. We aim to reply within one month.

Technical information

Civgraph is not a public sector body, so the UK and EU public sector accessibility regulations do not apply to it. We follow their model statement voluntarily, because readers recognise its layout.

Civgraph aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2, level AA. It is partially compliant, because of the problems listed below.

Problems we are fixing

These are the issues found in our audit of 11 November 2026 that are not yet fixed, by WCAG success criterion:

  • 1.4.1 Use of Color: some links inside sentences are identified by colour alone.
  • 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum): one selected-chip colour combination in the map catalogue.
  • 1.4.10 Reflow: one table on the Elections home page needs sideways scrolling at 400% zoom.
  • 2.4.3 Focus Order and 4.1.3 Status Messages: after opening a record in the PRONI search or the Elections pages, focus is not moved to the new content.
  • 2.4.7 Focus Visible: the map pages' skip links. In the PRONI search, focus on text boxes is shown only by a change of border colour.
  • 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum): in the map catalogue, the map's feature panel and the Browse vote lists.
  • 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum): some map controls.
  • 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value: the theme button does not report its state, the PRONI letter filter does not report the selected letter, and the PRONI record quick-view does not manage focus.

We are fixing these in order of impact, starting with the ones that affect every page.

Interactive maps

The map canvas cannot be made fully accessible to screen-reader users. We provide the same information as text through map search, each map's feature table in Browse and the election tables, and we are building a keyboard-navigable list of the features in view.

Content outside the scope of this statement

  • Scans of historical documents, maps and newspapers that we link to or show as sources. We provide the facts we take from them as accessible text and tables, but do not plan to make the scans themselves accessible.
  • Third-party content that we do not control: the base maps (OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap), the address search (Photon), the PRONI eCatalogue, the Internet Archive, and the support pages on Ko-fi and Substack.
  • Downloadable data files (CSV, GeoJSON, shapefiles, PMTiles). CSV files open in any spreadsheet or screen-reader-friendly tool.

How we tested this website

We tested civgraph.net on 11 November 2026, covering every type of page: the home page, the maps (with a layer and a feature selected), the classic catalogue, each kind of Browse record, Records and the Catalogue, PRONI search and records, the Census Explorer, Words in parliament, the Apps and About pages, and the "page not found" page.

Each page was tested at desktop width and at phone width (375 pixels), in the light and the dark theme: with automated checks (axe-core, against WCAG 2.2 A and AA); by keyboard alone; by inspecting what is exposed to screen readers (landmarks, headings, names, live regions, tables and chart alternatives); and for contrast, reduced motion, zoom to 200% and 400%, text spacing and target size. We have not yet listened to the site with a screen reader; that check comes next.
